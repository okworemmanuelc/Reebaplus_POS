- **2026-07-30** — **#209 the v2 sale envelope stops dropping the customer's crate credit (cloud `0173`)**: `pos_record_sale_v2` is server-authoritative on totals — it recomputes the gross from `p_items` and derives the payable itself — but it derived it from **one** client-sent reduction, `p_discount_kobo`. Folding them would have made `net_amount_kobo` right and the discount column wrong — a visible defect traded for an invisible one. It also gets **no `orders` column**: `net_amount_kobo` is the credit's entire effect, and a column would cost a Drift bump + sync-registry entry + pull-restore case for a figure the client re-derives; when a real credit ships and needs itemising, that is the change to make, with its own printed receipt line as `ReceiptTotals.totalKobo` already says. **The client DERIVES the value rather than taking an argument** — `OrdersDao.createOrder` computes `Σ(qty × unit_price_kobo) − discount_kobo − (total_amount_kobo − crate_deposit_paid_kobo)`, i.e. "the part of the payable the header does not otherwise explain" — so any future cart reduction rides the envelope automatically and this seam cannot silently drop one again, while the client still never asserts a total (the RPC stays the authority on gross and on every line's price).
